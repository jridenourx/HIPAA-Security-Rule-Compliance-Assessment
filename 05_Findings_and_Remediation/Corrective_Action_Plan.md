## 1. Purpose

This Corrective Action Plan (CAP) translates findings from the simulated HIPAA Security Rule compliance assessment into prioritized remediation activities.

The plan identifies recommended corrective actions, proposed ownership, target completion dates, validation requirements, and closure criteria.

The objectives are to:

- Address identified control deficiencies.
- Improve the design and operation of security safeguards.
- Strengthen documentation and evidence retention.
- Establish accountability for remediation activities.
- Reduce risks affecting electronic protected health information (ePHI).
- Provide a repeatable process for monitoring remediation progress.

All owners and target dates are proposed assignments within the fictional Northstar Health Plan scenario.

## 2. Remediation Prioritization

The plan uses the following prioritization approach:

| Priority | Definition | Target Completion |
|---|---|---|
| P1 – High | Significant deficiency or evidence gap requiring prioritized management attention. | Within 30–60 days |
| P2 – Moderate | Control weakness or documentation gap requiring planned remediation. | Within 60–90 days |
| P3 – Low | Routine improvement activity with relatively low potential impact. | As scheduled by management |

Target dates are proposed planning deadlines beginning November 3, 2026. Management may adjust dates based on available resources, system dependencies, and risk considerations.

## 5. Detailed Corrective Actions

### H-01-01 – Security Risk Analysis Not Updated for Material Environmental Changes

**Priority:** P1 – High  
**Proposed Owner:** Security Officer  
**Supporting Evidence:** E-01  
**Target Date:** November 30, 2026  
**Status:** Open

**Corrective Actions**

1. Update the security risk analysis to include the third-party claims analytics integration introduced in July 2026.
2. Document the integration's data flows, ePHI exposure, access permissions, system connections, and relevant third-party dependencies.
3. Identify threats and vulnerabilities associated with the integration.
4. Evaluate likelihood and impact of identified risks.
5. Document existing safeguards and residual risks.
6. Establish a process requiring risk analysis updates following material system, vendor, or business process changes.

**Required Remediation Evidence**

- Updated security risk analysis.
- Documented system and data flow information.
- Risk assessment results for the claims analytics integration.
- Management review or approval.
- Documented process for future risk analysis updates.

**Validation Criteria**

Confirm that the updated analysis includes the July 2026 integration, identifies relevant risks, evaluates safeguards, and documents management review.

## 7. Remediation Monitoring

The Security Officer should maintain the CAP as a centralized tracking document and provide periodic status updates to management.

Recommended reporting frequency:

| Activity | Frequency | Responsible Role |
|---|---|---|
| Review remediation progress | Biweekly | Security Officer |
| Review overdue actions | Monthly | Security Officer / Management |
| Review high-priority findings | Monthly | Management |
| Validate completed remediation | As actions are completed | Compliance Manager |
| Report residual risks | Monthly or as needed | Security Officer |

### 7.1 Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| Open | Corrective action has not started. |
| In Progress | Remediation activities are underway. |
| Pending Validation | Management reports remediation complete; evidence is awaiting independent validation. |
| Closed | Corrective action has been validated and closure criteria have been met. |
| Accepted Risk | Management has formally accepted the residual risk with documented approval and rationale. |

### 7.2 Closure Requirements

A finding should not be closed solely because a policy or procedure has been updated.

Before closure, the assessor or designated compliance reviewer should verify:

1. The corrective action addresses the identified condition.
2. Required documentation has been updated and approved.
3. Supporting evidence demonstrates implementation.
4. Relevant personnel have been informed or trained where applicable.
5. Operating evidence is available when the remediation requires ongoing execution.
6. Remaining risks or exceptions have been documented.
7. Closure approval has been recorded.

## 8. Management Reporting

The Security Officer should provide management with a periodic remediation summary containing:

- Total formal findings by severity.
- Number of findings open, in progress, pending validation, and closed.
- High-priority findings past target date.
- Significant remediation dependencies.
- Accepted risks and approval status.
- Corrective actions requiring additional resources.
- Progress toward validating control effectiveness.

## 9. Overall Remediation Strategy

Northstar should prioritize remediation in the following order:

**Priority 1 – Risk and Access Management**

Address the stale risk analysis, establish risk treatment ownership, improve workforce access management, and implement documented access reviews.

**Priority 2 – Technical Safeguards**

Validate access control configurations, authentication enforcement, audit logging coverage, and transmission security safeguards.

**Priority 3 – Resilience and Monitoring**

Validate backup restoration, improve activity review documentation, and strengthen workstation safeguard evidence.

**Priority 4 – Workforce and Process Improvements**

Improve workforce clearance procedures, training reconciliation, overdue training follow-up, and emergency access documentation.

**Priority 5 – Evidence and Validation**

Retain supporting evidence, validate corrective actions, and formally close findings only after remediation has been demonstrated.
