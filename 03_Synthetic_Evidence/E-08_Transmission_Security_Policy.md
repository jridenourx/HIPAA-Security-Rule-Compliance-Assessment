# E-08: Transmission Security Policy

**Organization:** Northstar Health Plan (Fictional)  
**Document Owner:** IT Security Manager  
**Document Type:** Technical Security Policy  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Last Review Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Classification:** Internal – Confidential  
**Assessment Reference:** HIPAA Security Rule §164.312(e)

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for protecting electronic protected health information (ePHI) transmitted across organizational networks and external electronic communications channels.

The objective is to protect ePHI against unauthorized access, interception, modification, or disclosure during electronic transmission.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to workforce members, systems, and third-party services involved in transmitting ePHI.

Covered transmission channels include:

- Cloud-hosted claims management platform communications.
- Organizational email and collaboration services.
- Remote workforce connections.
- Electronic communications with authorized business partners.
- Network connections between organizational systems and third-party service providers.

## 3. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| IT Security Manager | Oversees transmission security requirements and policy compliance. |
| IT Department | Maintains approved network security configurations and transmission safeguards. |
| Workforce Members | Use approved communication channels when transmitting sensitive information. |
| Cloud Service Provider | Maintains applicable transmission protections for hosted services. |
| Department Managers | Ensure workforce members understand approved communication practices. |

## 4. Approved Transmission Methods

Northstar requires workforce members to use approved systems and communication channels when transmitting ePHI.

Approved methods include:

- The cloud-hosted claims management platform.
- Organizational email services configured for approved sensitive information handling.
- Authorized remote access connections.
- Approved third-party services covered by applicable organizational agreements and security requirements.

Workforce members must not transmit ePHI through unauthorized personal email accounts or unapproved file-sharing services.

## 5. Encryption Requirements

Northstar relies on encryption capabilities provided by its approved systems and third-party service providers to protect ePHI during electronic transmission.

The organization expects sensitive information transmitted across external networks to be protected using appropriate encryption safeguards.

### 5.1 Cloud Platform Communications

The cloud-hosted claims management platform uses encrypted network connections to protect communications between workforce members and the application.

The IT department coordinates with the provider regarding the platform's transmission security configuration.

### 5.2 Remote Workforce Connections

Remote workforce members access organizational systems using approved authentication and remote access mechanisms.

The organization uses multifactor authentication for remote access to the claims management platform.

### 5.3 Electronic Mail

Organizational email services provide encryption capabilities for supported communications.

Workforce members are expected to follow established procedures when transmitting sensitive information through email.

**Identified Process Limitation:** Northstar has not maintained a complete inventory identifying all electronic transmission channels that may contain ePHI or documenting the encryption safeguards applied to each channel.

## 6. Transmission Integrity

Northstar relies on approved system and network security capabilities to protect transmitted information against unauthorized modification.

The IT department coordinates with service providers to maintain appropriate security configurations.

**Identified Process Limitation:** Documentation demonstrating that transmission integrity safeguards have been evaluated across all applicable communication channels is incomplete.

## 7. Third-Party Service Providers

Northstar relies on third-party service providers for cloud-hosted applications and supporting communication infrastructure.

The IT department is responsible for coordinating with providers regarding applicable transmission security safeguards.

Third-party arrangements involving ePHI must be evaluated for appropriate contractual and security requirements.

The organization expects applicable business associate relationships to be supported by appropriate agreements.

**Identified Process Limitation:** Documentation validating encryption configurations and transmission security responsibilities across all third-party integrations is incomplete.

## 8. Monitoring and Configuration Management

The IT department is responsible for coordinating the maintenance of approved transmission security configurations.

Security configuration changes should be reviewed by authorized personnel.

The IT Security Manager oversees policy compliance and coordinates corrective actions when transmission security deficiencies are identified.

**Identified Process Limitation:** Northstar does not consistently retain documentation demonstrating periodic validation of encryption configurations and supported communication protocols.

## 9. Workforce Responsibilities

Workforce members must:

- Use approved systems when transmitting ePHI.
- Avoid sending sensitive information through personal email accounts.
- Follow organizational secure communication procedures.
- Report suspected unauthorized disclosure of sensitive information.
- Report suspicious communications or potential interception events.
- Follow applicable security awareness training requirements.

## 10. Policy Review

The IT Security Manager is responsible for coordinating periodic reviews of this policy.

The policy should be updated when significant system, communication, or regulatory changes occur.

## 11. Assessment Considerations

The assessment should evaluate whether:

- Electronic transmission channels involving ePHI are identified.
- Appropriate transmission safeguards are implemented.
- Encryption capabilities are documented and validated.
- Transmission integrity safeguards are evaluated.
- Third-party transmission responsibilities are understood.
- Workforce members use approved communication channels.
- Supporting evidence demonstrates that safeguards are operating.

The presence of encryption capabilities within individual systems does not independently demonstrate that all applicable transmission channels are adequately protected.

## 12. Regulatory References

**HIPAA Security Rule:**

- 45 CFR §164.312(e)(1) – Transmission Security.
- §164.312(e)(2)(i) – Integrity Controls.
- §164.312(e)(2)(ii) – Encryption.

Both transmission security implementation specifications are designated Addressable and must be evaluated for reasonableness and appropriateness.

**Supporting Guidance:** NIST SP 800-66 Rev. 2, Implementing the HIPAA Security Rule: A Cybersecurity Resource Guide.
